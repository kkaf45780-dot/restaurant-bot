# Demo environment data

This Supabase project currently represents a **demo restaurant**, not a real business.

Restaurant ID: `rest_meshweyat_elhara`
Demo name: `مشويات الحارة — مطعم تجريبي`

Seeded data currently includes:
- 5 menu categories
- 16 sample menu items (illustrative EGP prices only)
- 3 delivery zones with fictional fees
- 1 demo coupon (`DEMO10`)
- 3 fictional customers with placeholder phone numbers
- 3 demo orders (`DEMO1001`, `DEMO1002`, `DEMO1003`) and 6 order-item rows

All demo orders include a note that they must not be prepared or delivered. They are for dashboard, status, and analytics testing only. Do not treat demo menu prices, zones, addresses, phones, or sales as real restaurant data.

When replacing this demo with a real restaurant, clear or replace the demo records and confirm menu pricing, contact details, delivery areas, opening hours, and payment settings with the business owner first.
